# The Blue Hour: brief (draft)

**The idea.** A noir game set in one Art Deco jazz club, one night in 1929. This first look is only the place: no game yet.

**What it's going for.**

- A look that is the point: flat cel-shaded graphic noir, with hard bands of light, gold line work and one colour key per room.
- A room that feels lived in: a band on a spot-lit stage playing real 1920s recordings, guests at their tables, a barman, smoke rising from ashtrays.
- Three places with their own mood: the amber hall, a card room under blue and violet lamps, and a balcony over the night city.

**What it isn't.** Yet: a game. There are no goals, characters to talk to or story so far.
