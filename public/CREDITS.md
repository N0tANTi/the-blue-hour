## Made in code

The club itself is built in code from flat colours: walls, stage, proscenium, curtain, columns, the gold line work, the bar, bottles, lanterns, booths, tables, lamps, glasses, the smoking stand, the card room, the balcony, the stained glass and the smoke. So is the city: towers, river reflections, the bridge, the moon and the searchlights. The band's playing is hand-keyed in code.
